'The Carrot Seed That Took Its Time
Luna was a little white rabbit who loved gardening. One sunny spring morning,
she carefully planted a tiny carrot seed in the soft soil of her backyard
Every day after that, she watered the spot and leaned down to watch. But day after day, the dirt stayed flat and empty.
Nothing green poked through.
"I think it’s dead," Luna said with a sigh, ready to dig the seed up.
Just then, her best friend Squirrel hopped over. "Hold on," he said. "Seeds don’t rush.
They grow under the ground first, where you can’t see them.'
